## Getting Started

PartyScout is a 5-step wizard that helps you find birthday party venues based on:
- Your child's age
- Party type preferences
- Budget
- Location

**Access the app**: https://partyscout-frontend-869352526308.us-central1.run.app

---

## Step 1: Child Information

### What to Enter

| Field | Description | Required |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| Child's Name | Used to personalize the experience | No |
| Age | Helps suggest age-appropriate party types | Yes |
| Party Date | When you're planning the party | Yes |

### Tips

- **Age matters**: Different ages have different party type options
  - Ages 2-4: Characters, play cafes
  - Ages 5-10: Most options available
  - Ages 11+: Amusement, outdoor adventures

- **Date selection**: Choose a date at least 2-3 weeks out to ensure venue availability

---

## Step 2: Party Preferences

### Party Types

Choose one or more party types:

| Type | Best For | Examples |
|------|----------|----------|
| **Active Play** | High-energy kids | Trampoline parks, gymnastics, skating, swimming |
| **Creative** | Artistic kids | Art studios, pottery, cooking classes, science |
| **Amusement** | Game lovers | Arcades, movies, escape rooms, bowling, laser tag |
| **Outdoor** | Nature lovers | Parks, zoos, farms, adventure parks |
| **Characters & Performers** | Young children | Magicians, princesses, superheroes |
| **Social & Dining** | Casual gatherings | Restaurants, cafes, pizza parties |

### Guest Count

Enter the number of children attending (not including adults).

**Tip**: Most venues have capacity limits. Be accurate to get the best matches.

### Budget

Use the slider to set your budget range.

**What's included in the estimate**:
- Venue rental/admission
- Basic party package
- Does NOT include: food, cake, decorations, favors

---

## Step 3: Location

### ZIP Code

Enter your ZIP code or the area where you want the party.

### Indoor/Outdoor Preference

| Option | When to Choose |
|--------|----------------|
| Indoor | Weather concerns, winter parties, controlled environment |
| Outdoor | Summer parties, nature themes, large groups |
| Any | Maximum venue options |

### Distance

How far are you willing to travel? Options: 5, 10, 15, 20, or 25 miles.

**Tip**: Larger distance = more options, but consider guest convenience.

## Step 4: Venue Results

### Understanding the Results

Each venue card shows:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│  [Venue Name]           87/100  │  ← Match Score
│  ★ 4.5 (234 reviews)           │  ← Google Rating
│  2.3 miles away                │  ← Distance
│  Indoor                        │  ← Setting
│                                │
│  Estimated: $350               │  ← Total Cost
│  ($23/person)                  │  ← Per Person
│                                │
│  [Select]  [Compare]           │  ← Actions
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Match Score (0-100)

Higher is better! The score is based on:
- Age appropriateness (25 points)
- Budget fit (25 points)
- Can handle your guest count (20 points)
- Distance (15 points)
- Google rating (10 points)
- Party type match (5 points)

### Filtering & Sorting

**Filter by**:
- All venues
- Indoor only
- Outdoor only
- 4+ star rating

**Sort by**:
- Best Match (recommended)
- Highest Rated
- Closest
- Lowest Price

### Compare Mode

1. Click "Compare" on 2-3 venues
2. Click "Compare (2)" or "Compare (3)" button
3. See side-by-side comparison
4. Select the winner

## Tips for Success

### 1. Book Early
Popular venues book up 4-6 weeks in advance, especially for weekends.

### 2. Ask Questions
When calling venues, ask:
- Is the date available?
- What's included in the party package?
- What's the deposit?
- What's the cancellation policy?

### 3. Consider Your Child
- Active kids → Active Play, Outdoor
- Creative kids → Creative
- Shy kids → Smaller venues, Characters at home
- Mixed group → Amusement (something for everyone)

### 4. Think About Parents
- Will parents stay? Need seating?
- Is there parking?
- Is it easy to find?

### 5. Plan for Extras
Budget for:
- Cake ($30-100)
- Decorations ($20-50)
- Goody bags ($3-5 per child)
- Food if not included ($5-15 per child)

## FAQ

### Q: How accurate are the prices?

**A**: Prices are estimates based on typical party packages. Actual prices may vary. Always confirm with the venue.

### Q: Why isn't my favorite venue showing up?

**A**: We use Google Places data. If a venue is:
- Very new
- Not listed on Google
- Outside your distance range
- Doesn't match your party type

It may not appear. Try expanding your search criteria.

### Q: Can I save my search?

**A**: Not yet! This feature is coming soon. For now, take screenshots or note your favorite venues.

### Q: How do I book a venue?

**A**: PartyScout helps you find venues, but booking is done directly with the venue. Use the contact information in Step 5 to call or visit their website.

### Q: What if I need to change my selections?

**A**: Use the "Back" button at the top of each step to go back and make changes.

### Q: Is my information saved?

**A**: No, we don't store any personal information. Your search is private and not saved.
