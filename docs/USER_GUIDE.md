## Getting Started

PartyScout helps you find venues for any party or event — birthdays, graduations, corporate events, baby showers, quinceañeras, and more — for any age group and any indoor or outdoor preference.

**Access the app**: https://partyscout.app

You can find venues in three ways:
1. **Landing Page** — quick search with a city + description
2. **AI Chat (Scout)** — conversational assistant that gathers details and shows venues inline
3. **Wizard** — guided 5-step flow with detailed preferences

---

## Landing Page Search

The landing page has a unified search bar:

```
[ City or ZIP ] [ Guests ] | [ Describe the party ] | [ Find → ]
```

- Enter a **city** (e.g. "Boston") and **guest count**
- Optionally describe the party (e.g. "outdoor birthday for an 8-year-old")
- Click **Find** to go straight to venue results

**Idea cards** below the search bar offer quick inspiration (e.g. "Outdoor Adventure", "Creative Studio", "Bowling Party") — click any card to pre-fill the search.

---

## AI Chat (Scout)

Click **Chat with Scout** on the landing page to open the AI chat panel.

Scout is a conversational assistant that helps you find venues by asking natural questions. Just describe what you're looking for:

> "I'm planning an outdoor graduation party for 30 people in Austin"

Scout will:
1. Extract details (city, occasion, age/persona, indoor/outdoor, themes)
2. Search Google Places in parallel for matching venues
3. Stream a short conversational reply
4. Show **venue cards inline** with photos, ratings, and links

### Chat Tips
- If Scout needs more info, it will ask **one question at a time**
- Mention "outdoor" or "inside" to control venue type
- Say the city clearly — Scout needs a city to search
- You can ask follow-up questions about the venues shown (e.g. "Which one is best for toddlers?")

## Wizard: Step by Step

Click **Plan with Wizard** or start from a venue result to enter the guided flow.

### Step 1: Child / Guest of Honor Info

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Name | Used to personalize the experience |
| Age | Helps suggest age-appropriate party types |
| Party Date | When you're planning the party |

**Tip**: Age determines which party types and venues are suggested.

---

### Step 2: Party Preferences

**Party Types** — choose up to 3:

| Type | Best For | Examples |
|------|----------|----------|
| Active Play | High-energy kids | Trampoline parks, gymnastics, skating, swimming |
| Creative | Artistic guests | Art studios, pottery, cooking classes, science |
| Amusement | Game lovers | Arcades, movies, escape rooms, bowling |
| Outdoor | Nature lovers | Parks, zoos, farms, adventure parks |
| Characters & Performers | Young children | Magicians, princesses, superheroes |
| Social & Dining | Casual gatherings | Restaurants, cafes, pizza parties |

**Guest Count** — enter the number of guests expected.

**Budget** — use the slider to set your budget range. The estimate covers venue rental / basic package; food, cake, and decorations are separate.

---

### Step 3: Location

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| City | Where you want the party (e.g. "Boston, MA") |
| Indoor/Outdoor | Indoor, Outdoor, or Any |
| Distance | How far you're willing to travel (5–25 miles) |

**Tip**: Selecting "Outdoor" searches using outdoor-specific venue queries (parks, farms, gardens, open-air spaces) so you don't see laser tag in your results.

---

### Step 4: Venue Results

Each venue card shows:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────┐
│  [Photo]                  87/100   │  ← Match Score
│  Sky Zone Trampoline Park          │
│  ★ 4.5 · 2.3 mi · Indoor          │
│                                    │
│  Est. $350  ($23/person)           │
│                                    │
│  [Select]  [Compare]  [Save]       │
└────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Filter chips** at the top: All / Indoor / Outdoor / 4+ Stars

**Sort chips**: Best Match / Highest Rated / Closest / Lowest Price

**Compare Mode**: Click Compare on 2–3 venues, then click the Compare button to see a side-by-side breakdown.

**Save**: Sign in to save a venue to your Saved Events list.

---

### Step 5: Party Details

After selecting a venue you'll see:

- **What's Included** — things the venue provides (jump time, party room, host)
- **What You'll Need to Bring** — food, cake, decorations, goody bags
- **Suggested Add-Ons** — optional upgrades with estimated cost
- **Contact Info** — phone + website to book directly

**Tip**: Mention you found them on PartyScout and tell them your guest count upfront.

## Saved Events

Sign in (top right) to save venues for later.

1. Click **Save** on any venue card
2. Fill in event date, guest count, and party types in the modal
3. Click **Save Event**

View all your saved events from the **Saved Events** link in the nav. You can delete events you no longer need.

## Sending Feedback

Click **Feedback** in the nav or footer to open the feedback form. If you're signed in, your name and email are pre-filled. Your message is sent to the PartyScout team and you'll receive an auto-reply confirmation.

## Tips for Success

### 1. Book Early
Popular venues book up 4–6 weeks in advance, especially weekends.

### 2. Ask the Right Questions When Calling
- Is the date available?
- What's included in the party package?
- What's the deposit and cancellation policy?
- Is there a minimum guest count?

### 3. Match the Venue to Your Child
- Active / high-energy → Active Play or Outdoor
- Creative / artistic → Creative
- Mixed group → Amusement (something for everyone)
- Very young (under 4) → Characters & Performers, soft-play venues

### 4. Think About Adults
- Will parents stay? Is there seating?
- Parking situation?
- Easy to navigate?

### 5. Budget for Extras
- Cake: $30–100
- Decorations: $20–50
- Goody bags: $3–5 per child
- Food (if not included): $5–15 per child

## FAQ

### Q: How accurate are the prices?
Prices are estimates based on typical party packages. Always confirm the final price with the venue.

### Q: Why isn't a specific venue showing up?
We use Google Places data. If a venue is very new, not listed on Google, outside your distance, or doesn't match your party type it may not appear. Try expanding distance or changing party type.

### Q: The outdoor filter shows no results — what do I do?
Try setting the distance to 20+ miles, or switch to "Any" setting and manually look for outdoor venues in the results.

### Q: Can I save my search without signing in?
No — saved events require a Firebase account (Google or email). The wizard search itself is always public.

### Q: How do I book a venue?
PartyScout finds venues; booking is done directly with the venue. Use the phone number or website in Step 5.

### Q: Is my information stored?
Your Firebase account email and saved events are stored securely. Search queries are not tied to your identity.

### Q: What events does Scout support?
Scout handles any event type — birthdays, graduations, baby showers, bridal showers, anniversaries, corporate events, quinceañeras, bar/bat mitzvahs, holiday parties, and more.
